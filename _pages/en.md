I am a researcher at the [Media Ecosystem Observatory](https://meo.ca/) (McGill University), where I work on the 2026 Québec general election, and at the [UNESCO Chair in Prevention of Radicalisation and Violent Extremism](https://chaireunesco-prev.ca/) (UNESCO-PREV Chair) at Université de Sherbrooke, where I have worked since 2023. I am also a trainer at [Cégep de Sorel-Tracy](https://www.cegepst.qc.ca/). I hold a bachelor's degree in applied political studies (public policy track) from Université de Sherbrooke, where I am completing a master's degree in applied political studies.

My work focuses on foreign interference, information security, and the strategic uses of artificial intelligence. I am particularly interested in what circulates in the information ecosystem during elections: narratives that question the integrity of the vote, the actors who spread them, and how they reach voters. After the 2025 federal election, I am now following the 2026 Québec election campaign.

Much of this work relies on strategic monitoring and open-source intelligence: tracking news and alternative media, observing social media feeds, content analysis, and information mapping. I also build the tools that make this monitoring possible (automated collection pipelines in Python, SQL and Power Automate, incident reporting grids, and methods to structure analysis and the delivery of reports).

I contributed to the 2024 and 2025 annual reports of the UNESCO-PREV Chair and to the Media Ecosystem Observatory report [*The Canadian Information Ecosystem during the 2025 Federal Election*](https://mediatechdemocracy.com/en/publications/federal-election_2025/). I have also published analyses of international politics in [*Perspective Monde*](https://perspective.usherbrooke.ca/) (Université de Sherbrooke). A list of my publications is available [here](/recherche/) (in French).

At Cégep de Sorel-Tracy, I design and deliver training on the use of artificial intelligence in the workplace.

In 2024, I received the Desjardins Democracy Scholarship from the Fondation Jean-Charles-Bonenfant and an excellence mention for my bachelor's degree. I work in French, English, and Arabic.
